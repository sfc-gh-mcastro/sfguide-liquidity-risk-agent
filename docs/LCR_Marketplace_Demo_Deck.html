Sharing & Distribution

Share Agents Through
Snowflake Marketplace

Share your Cortex Agent — along with its semantic view and underlying data — with other Snowflake accounts. Consumers get an AI-ready data product out of the box.

What Gets Shared

  • Cortex Agent (orchestration + instructions)
  • Semantic View (business logic + relationships)
  • Underlying tables (live data access)
  • Verified queries (pre-validated SQL)

Consumer Experience

  • Install listing → agent appears in CoWork
  • Ask questions in natural language immediately
  • Choose their own warehouse for compute
  • No data movement, no ETL, no setup

How It Works

Three Steps to Share

  1. Create a Secure Share
    CREATE SHARE lcr_agent_share;
    GRANT USAGE ON DATABASE LIQUIDITY_RISK_DB TO SHARE lcr_agent_share;
    GRANT USAGE ON SCHEMA LIQUIDITY_RISK_DB.PUBLIC TO SHARE lcr_agent_share;
  2. Grant Agent + Semantic View to the Share
    GRANT USAGE ON AGENT LIQUIDITY_RISK_DB.PUBLIC.LIQUIDITY_FORECAST_AGENT TO SHARE lcr_agent_share;
    GRANT SELECT, REFERENCES ON SEMANTIC VIEW LIQUIDITY_RISK_DB.PUBLIC.LIQUIDITY_SV TO SHARE lcr_agent_share;
  3. Publish as a Listing (or Direct Share)

    Use Provider Studio in Snowsight to create a Marketplace listing, or share directly with specific accounts. Auto-fulfillment replicates to other regions automatically.
